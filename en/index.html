Excellence at your service

A real pastry chef for your events in Montreal

I'm Pascaline Camblong, pastry chef and founder of La Crème des Bordelaises. Trained in the kitchens of Gordon Ramsay and Philippe Etchebest, then a contestant on the French TV competition Le Meilleur Pâtissier – Les Professionnels, I have been sharing the secrets of French pastry since 2018. After teaching over 5,000 students in Bordeaux, the adventure continues today in Montreal and Greater Montreal.

My pastry workshops are for companies and individuals alike. For a team building activity or a corporate Christmas party, I come straight to your office, no kitchen needed. For a bachelorette party, a baby shower or a birthday with friends, I organize a private workshop at your home or at your party venue. And to build your skills, my private baking classes at home teach you the recipe of your choice, solo, as a pair or with family, with children from age 7.

Every workshop is turnkey : I bring the ingredients, equipment and aprons, and I guide you step by step, in French or English. You leave with your creation and the recipe sent by email, so you can make it again at home.

Frequently asked questions

Your pastry workshops in Montreal, in practice

Where do you travel?

Anywhere on the Island of Montreal, with no travel fees. I also travel to Laval, the South Shore, the North Shore and beyond, with travel fees stated in your quote.

Do we need an equipped kitchen?

No. For group workshops, a water source and tables are all you need: I bring all the equipment, ingredients and aprons. For private classes at home, we use your oven, so you can easily make the recipe again afterwards.

How many people can take part?

From 1 person for a private class up to large corporate groups: for more than 50 participants, I simply adapt the workshop format. Group workshops start at 4 participants.

Can the workshops be held in English?

Yes, I teach in French or in English, and I can switch between the two for bilingual groups.

Can a workshop be given as a gift?

Yes: a private baking class is an original gift idea for a birthday, Christmas or a retirement. Write to me and we'll prepare the gift card together.
